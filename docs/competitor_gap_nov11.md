# What other Ayurveda / wellness Instagram accounts do vs @ayurvision_02 (Nov 11 2026)
Method and limits: Instagram pages cannot be opened from here and vidIQ credits are too low (2 left, 5-10 needed per search), so this is built from public directories (Socialveins) and 2026 playbooks (FluxNote, ThePrint, BrandLoom). It shows patterns, not those accounts' private analytics. Competitor follower counts are from the directories (April-Nov 2026) and may have changed.

## Who is out there (examples from the directories)
- Doctor-led Ayurveda accounts: @dr.sharmarobin (3.0M, Ayurveda), @tulsiayurveda (186K, Ayurveda consultant, consults + products), @drdixa_healingsouls (214K, BAMS doctor, 1:1 email consults), @doctorrekha (215K, "4th-generation Ayurveda doctor", online consults + Agni program), @ayurvedic_healing (82K, reports 500+ patients), @_nitisheth_ (109K, "simple everyday Ayurveda", recipes).
- Brand: Kapiva (~680K, verified, "India's first modern Ayurvedic brand", app + shop link in bio). Himalaya uses creator-led explainers ("herbal benefits in simple language"). Vanvasi Ayurveda ran a templated, consistent look with a monthly seasonal + promo calendar (agency case study, no numbers).

## What they do that we do not
1. A real face and a named expert (BAMS / "4th generation" / "500+ patients"): trust is the product in health content. We use stock b-roll with an AI voice, no face, no credential.
2. Direct-to-camera, first-person, personal-story reels (15-45 s, "the biggest lie" style titles). We use montage reels.
3. A paid or owned next step: 1:1 consults, diet plans, programs, app/shop link. We only have "DM CHART / WhatsApp".
4. Daily two-way engagement: Stories with polls/Q&A, comment replies, commenting on 20-30 niche accounts a day. We post only reels and carousels, no Stories.
5. India-specific sharing loop: WhatsApp shares carry big weight, so they build "forward this" content and a WhatsApp community/channel. We use WhatsApp only as a number in the watermark.
6. Trending audio with text overlay (Indian reels are audio-driven). We use fixed stock music.
7. Collab reels with similar-size creators (advised from ~5K followers; not yet for us).
8. Consistent niche and bio: one clear promise, verified/link-in-bio setup. Our bio is unverified (profile blocked).

## What we already do like them
Hinglish, hook in 1-3 s, numbered lists with the number in frame 1, word-by-word captions, myth-busting and seasonal/cultural topics, 5 hashtags, CTA to share/save/comment.

## What we do differently (keep)
Soft "supports" wording, no cure claims, disclaimer on every CTA, no prices. The ThePrint ground report shows the risk in this niche is misleading claims; stay compliant.
